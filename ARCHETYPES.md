# RopeBridge Archetypes

RopeBridge has three core archetypes.

## Claim

RopeBridge Claim = "I want the thing."

Claim turns a QR scan into a concrete request for an offer, sample, pickup, download, reservation, or other immediate action.

## Handshake

RopeBridge Handshake = "I want the relationship."

Handshake is for relationship capture: booth scans, expo leads, vendor follow-up, and business card replacement.

## Remember

RopeBridge Remember = "Remember this for next time."

Remember is for preference continuity: usual orders, product preferences, grooming notes, service details, and specs that should be retrieved later.
